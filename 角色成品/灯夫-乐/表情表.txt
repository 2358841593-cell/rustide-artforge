A 1990s hand-inked cel animation expression sheet for the character in the reference image.
Six head-and-shoulders portraits of that same one character, laid out in a tidy grid of three
across and two down, evenly spaced on a plain white background.

SAME PERSON EVERY TIME: in all six cells the hair is identical in colour, length and shape —
short side-parted chestnut hair with one thick lock across the forehead; nothing covers the face stays exactly as it is; the collar and shoulders show
a pale blue shirt collar under an ash-grey knit and a moss-green coat in the same colours; a wide square jaw making the whole lower face a flat block appears in every cell exactly as it does in the reference.
Only the eyes, brows and mouth change from cell to cell.

THE SIX: top row — calm and level; open happy laughing; downcast and sad.
Bottom row — angry with the brows driven down; startled with the eyes blown wide;
puzzled with one brow up and the head tipped.

FACE RULES: each face stays a plain flat shape. Eyes are two eyes creased into gentle upward half-moons, redrawn for each emotion.
Brows are two straight brows sitting level. A short mouth line that changes shape with the emotion. Two round
hard-edged blush patches on every cell.

INK AND FILL: warm dark brown #2E241F hand-drawn brush lines, the outer contour heavy and
wandering, the lines inside thin and sparse. Every colour is one flat matte fill with a single
hard-edged shadow. Skin is pale pink-beige #F5E0BC with one shadow #DCC29B.
