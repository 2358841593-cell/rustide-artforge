A 1990s hand-inked cel animation colour key sheet for the character in the reference image.
On a plain white background: the same character's head and shoulders drawn once at the left,
and to the right of it a tidy vertical column of flat colour chips, each chip a plain square of one
solid colour. Beside every chip, hand-lettered in two lines: the colour's name on the first
line and its six-digit hex code on the second, written out in full with the # sign.

THE CHIPS, top to bottom: skin #F5E0BC · skin shadow #DCC29B ·
hair #24211C · hair shadow #3A342C · Deep-teal #27777F folded-open lapels and lining, the strongest colour on the figure ·
deep-navy #2F3542 · bone-white #F1DEA9 · tide-red #DD5233 · line #2E241F.

Each chip is one flat matte square of exactly that colour, hard-edged, with a thin warm dark
brown outline. **Every chip carries its hex code in the lettering beside it.** The head-and-shoulders drawing at the left uses those same colours.

INK AND FILL: warm dark brown #2E241F hand-drawn brush lines. Every colour is one flat matte
fill with a single hard-edged shadow. Light from upper left.
