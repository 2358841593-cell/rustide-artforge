A 1990s hand-inked cel animation pose sheet for the character in the reference image.
The same one character drawn four times in a row on a plain white background, evenly spaced,
each one a different full-body action pose, all the same height.

THE FOUR POSES: standing at rest · walking mid-stride · reaching up with one arm ·
crouched low working at something on the ground.

SAME PERSON EVERY TIME: every pose keeps the same proportions — big head, five heads tall,
short and compressed, a narrow light torso with sloping shoulders, thin plain dark legs, huge square boots. Hair is short thick ink-dark hair cut in blunt uneven lengths, a heavy ragged fringe hanging over the brows.
Clothing is the same garment throughout: a crisp white stand-collar shirt under a fitted charcoal waistcoat, a long double-breasted deep-navy assayer's coat with a wide storm-cape flap over the shoulders and huge lapels folded open to show a deep-teal lining, wide dark trousers into huge boots. the heavy ragged fringe, hanging as one solid dark slab across the whole upper face stays visible.
The same cluster of small details rides along: a slate touchstone slab, a single loupe on a neck cord, a rack of small reagent vials in an inner pocket.

The cloth swings and the hems flap differently in each pose, but every garment is still one
large unbroken field of a single colour.

INK AND FILL: warm dark brown #2E241F hand-drawn brush lines, the outer contour heavy and
wandering. Every colour is one flat matte fill with a single hard-edged shadow.
