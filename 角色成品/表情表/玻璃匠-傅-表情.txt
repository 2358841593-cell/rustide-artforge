A 1990s hand-inked cel animation expression sheet for the character in the reference image.
Six head-and-shoulders portraits of that same one character, laid out in a tidy grid of three
across and two down, evenly spaced on a plain white background.

SAME PERSON EVERY TIME: in all six cells the hair is identical in colour, length and shape —
thick ink-dark waves pushed back off the forehead; heavy square-framed spectacles and a full dark beard joining the moustache stays exactly as it is; the collar and shoulders show
a blue pin-striped shirt collar spread wide under a moss-green quilted wrap in the same colours; the square spectacle frames, each lens as wide as his cheek, sitting on the full beard appears in every cell exactly as it does in the reference.
Only the eyes, brows and mouth change from cell to cell.

THE SIX: top row — calm and level; open happy laughing; downcast and sad.
Bottom row — angry with the brows driven down; startled with the eyes blown wide;
puzzled with one brow up and the head tipped.

FACE RULES: each face stays a plain flat shape. Eyes are two long narrow eyes behind the square lenses with heavy upper lids, redrawn for each emotion.
Brows are two thick dark brows angled down toward the nose. A short mouth line that changes shape with the emotion. Two round
hard-edged blush patches on every cell.

INK AND FILL: warm dark brown #2E241F hand-drawn brush lines, the outer contour heavy and
wandering, the lines inside thin and sparse. Every colour is one flat matte fill with a single
hard-edged shadow. Skin is pale pink-beige #F2D99A with one shadow #D4B87E.
Hair #24211C. Collar #525B95 and #5A9146. Light from upper left.
