BUILD: big head, short stocky body, five heads tall. The upper body is wide and heavy; below the
hips he narrows sharply. The legs are two thin plain dark tubes, bare of any detail, and the

CURVE AND TEAR: every edge is a slightly uneven curve — sleeves bow, the jaw rounds. Every hem is
torn into a jagged zigzag, the collar flares open in sharp points, loose strips of cloth hang off
the sleeve and hem, and the hair ends in big sharp points. Nothing has a clean straight edge.

LINE: warm dark brown #2E241F brush lines drawn by hand with a loaded brush — the stroke swells
and thins as it travels, wobbles off true, and overshoots at corners. The outer silhouette line
is heavy and wandering; the few lines inside are thin, sparse and broken in places.

SKIN: pale pink-beige #F0C694 with one shadow #C69163. The skin stays pale.

FACE: bald with an ink-dark hair in solid lobes; an enormous square beard swallows the whole lower face. The beard alone is as wide as his shoulders.
The face is a plain flat shape: two simple dark eye shapes, a short mouth line, two round hard-edged blush patches, and nothing else on it. a single tiny pair of spectacles perched above the beard.
