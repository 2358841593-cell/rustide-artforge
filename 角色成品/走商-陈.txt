A 1990s hand-inked cel animation model sheet. Single full-body character, front view,
plain white background.

Same drawing style and proportions as the reference sheets. A different person.

BUILD: big head, short stocky body, five heads tall. The upper body is wide and heavy; below the
hips he narrows sharply. The legs are two thin plain dark tubes, bare of any detail, and the
boots are huge and square. Head as tall as boots. A warm loud Windrunner trader
counting a fistful of tide-notes.

CURVE AND TEAR: every edge is a slightly uneven curve — sleeves bow, the jaw rounds. Every hem is
torn into a jagged zigzag, the collar flares open in sharp points, loose strips of cloth hang off
the sleeve and hem, and the hair ends in big sharp points. Nothing has a clean straight edge.

LINE: warm dark brown #2E241F brush lines drawn by hand with a loaded brush — the stroke swells
and thins as it travels, wobbles off true, and overshoots at corners. The outer silhouette line
is heavy and wandering; the few lines inside are thin, sparse and broken in places.

SKIN: pale pink-beige #EBA24E with one shadow #C77F3C. The skin stays pale.

FACE: ink-dark slicked-back hair in solid lobes; a neat pointed goatee covers the chin. A wide crescent grin stretches nearly ear to ear.
The face is a plain flat shape: two simple dark eye shapes, a short mouth line, two round hard-edged blush patches, and nothing else on it. a tiny brass bell hangs from one earlobe.

CLOTHES (a scavenged version of what the photo wears): a plain bone-white tee, a many-pocketed moss-green road coat,
a bright tide-red quilted travel wrap over both shoulders, wide dark road trousers. The shoulder wrap flares out as wide as his outstretched arms.

BIG FIELDS: each garment reads as one large unbroken field of a single colour, the way a poster
is printed. The clutter sits in one small cluster on the chest only; sleeves, back, legs and
boots stay clean empty colour.

DETAIL: about ten small mismatched details packed into that one chest cluster — round badges and pins where each one
is a different colour from the one beside it — purple next to yellow next to blue next to orange, strips of tape, patches of different shapes, visible stitching, a clip,
a buckle. They crowd together there and the rest of the figure stays clean. He is a road trader, so he carries a bundle of tied paper notes, a strap hung with small bells, a rolled map tube.
Printed "W-6". The fistful of notes held up.
