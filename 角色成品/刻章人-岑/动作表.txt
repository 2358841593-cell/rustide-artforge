A 1990s hand-inked cel animation pose sheet for the character in the reference image.
The same one character drawn four times in a row on a plain white background, evenly spaced,
each one a different full-body action pose, all the same height.

THE FOUR POSES: standing at rest · walking mid-stride · reaching up with one arm ·
crouched low working at something on the ground.

SAME PERSON EVERY TIME: every pose keeps the same proportions — big head, five heads tall,
short and compressed, a narrow light torso with sloping shoulders, thin plain dark legs, huge square boots. Hair is very long dead-straight ink-dark hair falling past the elbows in solid lobes.
Clothing is the same garment throughout: a charcoal high-neck under a long dark sealcutter's gown with bound sleeves, a copper-orange quilted over-vest on top, narrow dark trousers into huge boots. two very thick straight brows reading as dark bars across the face stays visible.

The cloth swings and the hems flap differently in each pose, but every garment is still one
large unbroken field of a single colour.
