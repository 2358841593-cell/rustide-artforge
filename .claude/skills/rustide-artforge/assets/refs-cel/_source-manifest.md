# Eastward / 《风来之国》 cel-shaded / regular-anime official character art

This folder is **only** flat cel / clean anime character drawings.
Not painterly key art, not grainy countdown posters, not yellow comic posters, not murals, not pixel screenshots, not 3D site standees.

## Count

- **Previously here:** 10
- **New this pass:** 6
- **Total image files:** 16
- Plus `画风说明.md` (style note) and this manifest.

Honest limit: the public official **cel-shaded character** set is small. Wiki Supporting Art has exactly the 8 Early Art portraits + John+Sam standee already collected. No Lee / Maya / Uva / extra Early Art files exist on eastwardwiki.com. Official-site `characters/*.png` are 175×360 3D clay renders; splash standees are painterly/textured. Lost in Cult Design Works has no downloadable character model pages — Steam/LIC photos are pixel environments, Earthborn textured magazine covers, or tiny cassette-icon crops inside product shots. Catsuka hosts the opening video only. Japanese magazine / Pixpil tweet archives did not yield extra high-res cel sheets.

The only new high-res official cel material is Arnaud Tribout’s Souviens Ten-Zan **opening character-design posts** (tumblr `@triboutarn`, Sept 2021). One sheet there is a JPEG of the already-held `18-opening-character-designs.png` (same image, skipped). Pencil layouts (genga/layout, no cel color) and ~590px trailer stills were skipped as not matching “flat cel, high-res”.
